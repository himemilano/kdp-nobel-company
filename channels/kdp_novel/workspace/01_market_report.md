# Silver Creek Shifters Book 2 — Market Report (seed draft, 2026-11-28)

Prepared as the starting input for the Book 2 production cycle. The pipeline's
Market Researcher may refresh this file; the strategic points below are the
constraints the Plot Designer must honour.

## Series strategy

- Book 1 (The Alpha's Regret) proved the formula: rejected mate + groveling
  alpha + heroine who rebuilds herself stronger. Book 2 keeps the series brand
  (rejected mate at the core) but follows the Book 1 blueprint's own direction:
  "Book 2 starts from the single quiet thread in Chapter 54 — a new story, not
  a continuation of this arc."
- Series norm in this market: each volume follows a different couple inside the
  same world, with the previous couple appearing as honoured legacy figures.
  This protects Book 1's closed ending while giving returning readers a payoff.

## Market signals (checked 2026-11-28)

- Rejected mate remains a headline trope (e.g. "Rejected" by Jaymin Eve;
  "Rejected by the Alpha" by Diana Bold — rejected mate, second chance,
  groveling hero, strong heroine).
- Bestselling series volumes are fast reads of roughly 200–250 pages; series
  run 4–5+ volumes, largely read through Kindle Unlimited.
- Kindle ebook median price on the shifter shelf is $4.99 (paperback pricing
  for this series is fixed separately at $15.99 to match Book 1 / Tranquil
  Flora).
- High-performing trope stack for a Book 2: rejected mate, second chance,
  grovel, forced proximity, pack politics, a heroine with a hidden/awakening
  gift, found family. Secret-baby is a proven Book 2 trope in comparable
  series but is NOT used here (Book 1 already carried a hidden-secret line;
  repeating it would blur the volumes).

## Positioning for Book 2

- Setting: the southern pack beyond the barrier mountains — the land where
  Chapter 54's dormant thread trembled. New pack, new couple, same Loom
  mythology, wider world.
- The trembling thread is the heroine's bloodline waking up. Elena and
  Nicholas appear only as distant legends and in one mid-book contact scene;
  they must not steal the new couple's arc.
- Theme carried over from Book 1 (series promise): "The strongest thread is
  the one chosen freely."

## Hard rules carried over from Book 1 production lessons

1. The finale is designed in the blueprint from the start (final 4 chapters =
   resolution; epilogue ends in peace; at most ONE quiet series thread).
2. No repeated climax: each act's climax must be a different confrontation in
   a different place with different stakes (Book 1 looped one climax around
   chapters 40–42 — do not repeat that failure).
3. A foreshadowing ledger is kept in the blueprint appendix: every planted
   thread is listed and must be paid off or explicitly carried to Book 3.
4. Target: ~50–55 chapters, ~55,000 words, one chapter per pipeline run.
