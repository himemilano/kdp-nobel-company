# Chapter 40

The cloying stench of rot clung to the air, a thick blanket smothering the cavern. Around Nicholas, the corrupted descendants of the First Whisperers circled, their guttural snarls a horrifying chorus, their eyes a sickening emerald glow that promised only oblivion. At their head, Rafe-Serpent stood, a grotesque parody of his former self, nascent scales shimmering beneath reptilian skin. Nicholas, a living anchor to the Lifestone, fought a scorching internal battle, the Elder Serpent God’s poison burning in his veins. Yet, against the impossible tide, a grim resolve hardened his jaw.

Deep within the Lifestone, Elena’s consciousness, fused with the Seed-Crystal, perceived Nicholas’s torment as a searing spiritual current. Her inner sight pierced the veil of corruption, showing her the twisted legion not as mere flesh, but as a vast, parasitic web of raw, malevolent energy. Each thread of sickly emerald light converged on Rafe-Serpent, then spiderwebbed out to the tainted Lifestones across the world. This insidious connection, she knew, was their weakness.

A surge of the Seed-Crystal’s purifying current flowed from Elena to Nicholas. It was no complete cleansing, but a faint, insistent counter-pulse, peeling back the parasitic tendrils that sought to bind his Alpha will. Nicholas gasped, a harsh, burning agony ripping through him, but in that momentary, brutal liberation, his mind found a sharp, diamond-hard clarity.

Rafe-Serpent’s reptilian face stretched into a cruel, triumphant smile, half-obscured by nascent scales. The familiar timbre of Rafe’s voice, now a chilling blend with the Serpent’s dry hiss, cut through the din of the encroaching horde. "Foolish Alpha. Do you still believe the Whisperer saves? Her soul now feeds the world’s heart, a beautiful, agonizing sacrifice."

A wave of fractured memories, twisted and amplified by the Serpent God’s malice, crashed over Nicholas. He saw Elena’s face, contorted in despair on the night of her banishment, heard her broken cries of betrayal, each phantom sound a fresh torment, threatening to splinter his mind.

Within the Lifestone, Elena felt the insidious mental assault. The Elder Serpent God's malevolent echo throbbed with Rafe’s faint, rhythmic agony. This mental attack wasn't purely the Serpent’s will; it was driven by Rafe’s own tortured, weaponized memories. Elena poured the Seed-Crystal's pure healing light into Nicholas’s serpent-mark. It wasn't words, but a searing, undeniable knowledge that ripped through his being: *Rafe’s pain. That’s the core. Sever that link.*

Elena’s burning message ripped through Nicholas’s fractured awareness. He instantly perceived the core of Rafe-Serpent’s power—a faintly pulsing node on his friend’s chest, glowing with sickly emerald light. To strike there meant extinguishing the last, agonizing spark of Rafe’s soul. Nicholas’s jaw clenched, a low growl rumbling in his throat, hollowed by a loss too vast for words.

Rafe-Serpent's abyssal emerald eyes, choked with alien algae, flickered with a raw, unnerving blend of fear and the Serpent God’s cold fury. A piercing shriek tore from his throat, chilling and inhuman. "Strike, Alpha! Prove your love! Destroy your friend’s ghost, or watch your world perish!"

The corrupted horde surged, a hundred shadows lunging at Nicholas, their claws extended, teeth bared. He stood anchored to the Lifestone, battling the internal poison, his body wracked with pain. Rafe-Serpent’s mocking laughter echoed through the cavern, weaving into Elena’s desperate, silent command burning in his mind. Nicholas’s gaze, sharp as the crack of winter’s first ice, locked onto the pulsating node on Rafe-Serpent’s chest. He lifted his hand, poised to make the brutal, final decision, to sever the oldest, most painful bond.

*Would he sacrifice his friend to save the world? Or would his heart betray them all?*
